Paired GOF comparison of trace variants versus finite-order n-grams. Differences are trace variants minus finite order. Only logs eligible at both levels enter each comparison. Eligibility requires at least 50 fitted types. GOF non-rejection uses threshold 0.1. The folder also records comparison threshold 0.2, which does not enter the paired GOF outcome. The xmin policy is adaptive. Error bars are pointwise 95 percent Newcombe confidence intervals for paired proportions. Filled points indicate two-sided exact McNemar p-values below 0.05 after Holm adjustment for the planned comparison family of abstraction orders 1 through 20. N gives the number of paired logs. NA indicates an unavailable estimate. The intervals are pointwise. An interval that excludes zero need not remain significant after Holm adjustment.
